# Bugs

Found something that appears broken or doesn't behave the way you'd expect? Add it here.

Keep it simple — just tell us what happened and what you expected instead. Put any screenshots in `attachments/` and embed them below the bug they belong to.

You don't need to include severity, priority, IDs, browser info, or formal reproduction steps. If you happen to know something extra that seems useful, feel free to mention it naturally — but it's not required.

## Example

### Login button doesn't work

When I click **Sign in**, nothing happens.

I expected the login form to open.
