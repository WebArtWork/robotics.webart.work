# Feedback

Thank you for helping us improve this software! This folder is where we collect real, honest feedback from people who try it out.

**You do not need any technical knowledge to do this.** Just write naturally, like you're telling a friend what you thought.

## How it works

1. Fork this repository.
2. Clone your fork locally.
3. Open the `feedback` folder as an Obsidian vault.
4. Copy the entire `feedback/template` folder into `feedback/reviews`.
5. Rename the copied folder using your full name.
   Example:
   `feedback/reviews/John Smith/`
6. Fill in `Feedback.md` with your experience, opinions, ideas and suggestions.
7. Fill in `Bugs.md` with things that did not work correctly.
8. Add any relevant screenshots to your `attachments/` folder.
9. Embed screenshots in the Markdown files where they are relevant.
10. Remove the example content and `sample.png` when you're finished.
11. Commit your feedback to your fork.
12. Open a Pull Request back to this repository.

## What we're looking for

We want your honest reactions — things you liked, disliked, found confusing, expected to exist, ideas you had, and anything that felt wrong. There's no wrong answer here.

You don't need to complete every section. If a section doesn't apply to you, just remove it.

**Please don't** classify, prioritize, assign severity, create issue IDs, or write developer-style tickets. That's not your job — AI agents will take your raw feedback and structure it into actionable work later.

Just write what you actually think. That's all we need.
